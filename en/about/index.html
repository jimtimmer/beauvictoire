About Stephanie

My name is Stephanie Timmerman-Hongerbron. I was born and raised in Rotterdam, a wife, the mother of three grown-up children and the grandmother of three grandchildren.

I have been self-employed since 2000. I started as a medical pedicurist, skin therapist and beautician and grew my business into an accredited training company. In 2013 I founded Beau Victoire Thuiszorg, first as a certified care assistant (Verzorgende IG). In 2018 I qualified as a nurse, and in 2024 I completed my training as a quality nurse.

During the COVID-19 pandemic I worked as a nurse on the front line. Today I focus on 24-hour care, day care, night care and palliative care, and I train colleagues in care.

“For me, care is more than tasks. It is care for body, mind and spirit.”

I am a Christian, a minister's daughter and a gospel singer. My father passed on to me his love of caring for others. My faith is the source of how I work: with attention, patience and respect.

That is why I offer pastoral care on request. At the same time, everyone is welcome, whatever their faith or background. I speak Dutch, English, Spanish, French and German.
