Training and placements

Beau Victoire Thuiszorg is an accredited training company (Erkend Leerbedrijf). We supervise students on placement and train care professionals, one-to-one and in small groups.

Training for care professionals

Stay qualified and competent, and keep your skills up to date. We provide training in clinical nursing skills for self-employed carers and care professionals in training.

  • One-to-one lessons or no more than 2 people per session
  • Practice on a training manikin in a safe setting
  • In cooperation with other disciplines
