Care at home

Beau Victoire Thuiszorg is a private home care provider. You stay in control: you help decide when, how and for how long care is given. At home, and if you wish at your holiday address abroad.

24-hour care, including for several days

With 24-hour care you can go on living independently at home for longer, even if you need care day and night. It also gives family members and informal carers a chance to catch their breath.

  • Nursing and personal care in line with the Dutch BIG Act
  • Clinical nursing tasks, such as wound care, compression bandaging, catheter and stoma care
  • Help with shopping, meals and a walk outside
  • Attention to hygiene and your dietary wishes
  • Care at your holiday address, in the Netherlands and abroad

Block care, day care and night care

Don't need 24-hour care? Then we come for a fixed number of hours.

Block careDaytime, from 5 hours
Day careDaytime, from 8 hours
Night care8, 10 or 12 hours a night

Palliative and end-of-life care

Palliative care is care for people whose illness can no longer be cured, for example with cancer, dementia, or heart or kidney failure. It is care for body, mind and spirit.

  • 24-hour, day or night care, so that you get through the day as comfortably as possible
  • One-to-one night watches at your home
  • Close contact with your loved ones and your GP or treating doctor
  • If you wish, last offices, together with your loved ones

Pastoral care and aftercare

Would you or a loved one like a prayer or spiritual support in the last phase of life? You can let us know during the introduction. After a death, loved ones can also turn to us for a listening ear, a conversation or a referral to bereavement support.

We only offer pastoral care on request. Everyone is welcome, whatever their faith or background.

Oncological foot care and medical pedicure

Stephanie is officially registered as an oncological foot care provider (OVV), registration number 200114. Oncological foot care is specialised foot care for people who are having, or have had, chemotherapy. We always coordinate treatment with your treating doctor or oncology nurse.

Not under treatment? You can also make use of our medical pedicure, for example if you have diabetes or rheumatism. When you take blood thinners, even a small wound can have serious consequences, so expert foot care matters.

More about oncological foot care at oncofoot.com

Wigs and hair care

Illness or treatment can cause hair loss, and your hair gives you confidence. Through trusted hairdressers we supply made-to-measure hairpieces, wigs and human hair lace wigs, for men and women, for European and Black hair.

We can also cut and care for your hair at home.

Health check at home

Would you like to know whether your values are in order? We visit you at home for a health check, for example if you are gaining or losing weight quickly, or if you feel listless.

  • Blood pressure
  • Blood sugar
  • Weight
  • You can discuss the results with your GP or treating doctor

Costs and payment

We are a private home care provider. You pay by invoice after the care has been provided. In consultation, payment from a Dutch personal care budget (PGB) is also possible.

The cost depends on the number of hours and the type of care. Get in touch and we will make you a tailored proposal.

Frequently asked questions

How do I pay for care?

You receive an invoice after the care has been provided. Payment from a PGB is possible in consultation.
